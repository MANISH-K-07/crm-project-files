The four safeguards
  • Data-leak blocking (DLP): software that stops sensitive data, like account numbers, from leaving the company.
  • Behavior monitoring (UEBA): tools that learn what normal looks like and flag unusual activity.
  • Access control (PAM/IAM): rules for who can reach what, with extra locks on the most powerful accounts.
  • Staff reporting: hotlines where employees can raise concerns.

Are humans actually supervising the agent?

Agents often ask a person “may I do this?” before acting. If people click approve without reading, that safeguard is empty, and none of the four safeguards checks for it. This detector scores a reviewer’s log on speed, long streaks, and whether risky actions got the same quick glance as trivial ones.
